3. פניות בנושא נגישות ורכז נגישות: אם נתקלתם בקושי או בליקוי נגישות, נשמח לקבל את פנייתכם ולתקן בהקדם:

Accessibility Statement for Job Tracker Cloud

At Job Tracker Cloud, we are committed to providing an accessible, inclusive, and seamless experience for all users, including people with disabilities.


1. Accessibility Standards: This website was built and adapted in accordance with standard web accessibility requirements, adhering to WCAG 2.1 Level AA recommendations and applicable accessibility regulations.


2. Adjustments Made on the Site:

  • Full keyboard navigation support (Tab, Enter, Escape to close modals).
  • Full bidirectional text support (RTL for Hebrew, LTR for English).
